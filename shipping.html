Shipping & Returns

How your card is packaged, shipped, and protected

Processing time

Orders are dispatched within 1–2 business days of payment clearing.

Shipping regions & cost

We currently ship to the United Kingdom only. Shipping is a flat rate of £10 per order, covering tracked, signed-for, insured delivery.

Packaging

Every graded slab is shipped in a rigid mailer with additional padding to prevent movement and damage in transit, inside a plain, unbranded outer box. We photograph each card immediately before it's boxed, as a record of its condition at dispatch.

Tracking & signature

All orders are sent with tracked, signed-for delivery. You'll receive a tracking number by email once your order ships.

Tamper-evident seal

Every parcel is closed with a numbered, tamper-evident seal, and that seal number is included in your shipping email. Before accepting delivery, check the seal is intact and its number matches the one in your email. If the seal is broken or the number doesn't match, refuse the parcel if you can — or photograph it before opening — and contact us within 48 hours.

Returns

Because each card is a unique, individually graded item, all sales are final once shipped, except where a card arrives materially different from its listing (e.g. damaged in transit, or the slab/cert does not match the description). In that case, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos and we'll work with you on a resolution. If a return is agreed, items should be sent back to:

Authenticity guarantee

If your card's cert number ever fails to verify on PSA's or ACE's website, or doesn't match the card in the slab, contact us, send the card back to the address above, and we'll refund you in full. This isn't limited to the 48-hour window above.

Lost or damaged in transit

All shipments are insured for their full sale value. If your order arrives damaged or doesn't arrive, contact us immediately so we can file a claim and make it right.
