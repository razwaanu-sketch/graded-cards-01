About Us

Who we are and what we sell

Our story

Graded Cards 01 is a UK-based collector selling authenticated, professionally graded Pokémon cards directly to other collectors and investors. Every card we list has been graded by a reputable third-party grading company — such as PSA (Professional Sports Authenticator) or ACE Grading — and can be verified independently using the certification number shown on each listing.

What we sell

Our current collection is a set of Gem Mint 10 graded Japanese Special Art Rare and Mega Evolution Pokémon cards. Once a card is sold, its listing is removed.

Verifying a grade

You can verify any card's grade and certification number directly with the grading company shown on its listing — for PSA-graded cards, use PSA's official cert lookup; for ACE-graded cards, use ACE Grading's own certificate verification tool.

Contact

Questions about a listing, grading, or an order? Get in touch — we're happy to help.
