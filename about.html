About Us

Who we are and what we sell

Our story

Graded Cards 01 is a UK-based collector selling authenticated, professionally graded Pokémon cards directly to other collectors and investors. Every card we list has been graded by PSA (Professional Sports Authenticator) and can be verified independently using the certification number shown on each listing.

What we sell

Our current collection is a set of PSA 10 Gem Mint Japanese Special Art Rare Eeveelutions from the 2024 Pokémon SV8a set. Each card is a unique, 1-of-1 item — once it's sold, the listing is removed.

Verifying a grade

You can verify any card's grade and certification number directly on PSA's official cert lookup before or after purchase.

Questions about a listing, grading, or an order? Get in touch — we're happy to help.
